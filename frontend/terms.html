LEARNVAULT • TERMS

Use LearnVault responsibly.

These project-level terms describe expected platform use. Institutional deployments should replace them with their approved terms before public production use.

ACCEPTABLE USE

Use LearnVault for learning

Users should use LearnVault for authorised academic and educational activities and must not misuse accounts, content, assessments or platform access.

CONTENT

Learning materials

Users and administrators are responsible for ensuring uploaded or linked materials are appropriate and authorised for educational use.

ACCESS

Role permissions

Student, Faculty and Admin capabilities are role-controlled. Attempts to bypass permissions or access another user's protected data are prohibited.
